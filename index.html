The person behind the smart move

the future in every home.

Sheila Mae Fajutagana

Financially Savvy Realtor

Sheila Mae Fajutagana is a Financially Savvy Realtor who helps Filipinos turn their income into assets.

Most people start their property search by asking, “Can I afford a house?” Sheila starts with a better question: “What will this house do for your future?” For her, the goal is not just to own a home. The goal is to build wealth, and she helps her clients see the difference before they sign anything.

She works mostly with young professionals, OFWs, seafarers, and business owners. These are hardworking people who earn well but often don’t know where to begin, especially when they’re working long hours or living far from home. Sheila meets them where they are. She helps them understand their budget, their cash flow, and their financing options, whether through Pag-IBIG or a bank, so they can make a smart decision they won’t regret.

Every client starts with a free property assessment and a sample computation. No pressure and no guessing. You see the real numbers first, then decide what fits your income, your goals, and your family.

What people appreciate most is that she explains things in plain language. She answers the questions many buyers feel shy to ask, and she stays with you from your very first message until you hold the keys to your home. She doesn’t treat you like a sale. She treats you like someone she wants to see win.

Whether you’re buying your first home, investing from overseas, or building something for your children, Sheila helps you make moves that are smart today and secure for years to come.
